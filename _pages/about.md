I’m Waner (Arina) Zheng, a Financial Engineering Master’s student at [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/), with a strong foundation in quantitative research and algorithmic trading.

My past internships include roles at Soochow Securities and Zhejiang Zheqi Industrial, where I worked on factor construction, options strategies, and high-frequency backtesting. I developed models using Python and SQL, enhancing long-short equity and volatility arbitrage strategies.

At Cornell, I continue to deepen my skills in stochastic calculus, optimization, and machine learning, with a focus on financial applications. I’m passionate about designing data-driven trading solutions and understanding market microstructure.
